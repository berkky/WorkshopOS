# WorkshopOS Commercial Buyer FAQ

**Audience:** Prospective licensed customers and their technical/legal reviewers.  
**Status:** Informational — not a contract and not legal advice.  
**Product version:** v1.0.0-rc1 technical release candidate context.

Final rights are defined only in the executed commercial license agreement.

---

## Do I receive source code?

**Yes**, under the intended **WorkshopOS Commercial Source Edition** model, licensed customers receive source code for the licensed release, subject to confidentiality and use restrictions in the signed agreement.

## Can I customize it?

**Yes, for licensed internal use** is the preferred commercial position. You may modify the source to support your operations within your licensed scope. Redistribution and public publication remain restricted unless separately agreed.

## Can I use it forever?

**Perpetual use** of the **specific licensed version(s) delivered** is the preferred model. Future updates and major releases are generally covered by separate maintenance or upgrade entitlements.

## Do I own WorkshopOS?

**No.** WorkshopOS intellectual property remains with the licensor. You receive contractual usage rights, not ownership of the WorkshopOS product.

## Can I sell WorkshopOS?

**Not under the standard license.** Resale, sublicensing, and offering WorkshopOS as your own standalone product to unrelated customers require separate written commercial terms.

## Can I put it on public GitHub?

**No** for proprietary delivered source under the preferred model. Public repositories of WorkshopOS proprietary source are not permitted. Private source hosting within your organization may be allowed subject to confidentiality and security obligations.

## Can my developer or agency work on it?

**Yes**, when reasonably required for your licensed operations, provided they are bound by confidentiality and license restrictions (typically via your contractor agreements).

## Are updates included forever?

**Not by default.** Updates and upgrades are preferred as a separate **maintenance** entitlement that may be renewed commercially.

## Can I white-label it?

**Not in the standard Commercial Source Edition.** White-label and trademark modification rights require a **separate written agreement**.

## Who owns my data?

**You retain rights to your operational business data**, subject to your agreement and applicable law. WorkshopOS software ownership does not transfer ownership of your customer, vehicle, job, or financial records to the licensor.

## Does it include third-party software?

**Yes.** WorkshopOS includes third-party components under their own licenses (e.g., .NET/MIT, Bootstrap/MIT, jQuery/MIT, PostgreSQL driver license, DM Sans/SIL OFL). See [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

## Does it process card payments?

**No.** WorkshopOS records workshop-entered payment metadata only. It does not include a payment gateway and is not PCI card-processing scope.

## Is it tax / e-invoice compliant?

**No product guarantee.** WorkshopOS does not provide tax/VAT engines or legal e-invoice compliance. Operators remain responsible for local regulatory obligations.

## Can I run it on Windows?

**Source is cross-platform .NET 10**, but native Windows runtime verification was **not completed** in the v1.0.0-rc1 technical RC cycle. macOS build/test/publish was verified. Evaluate Windows deployment in your environment.

## Can I host it myself?

**Yes** — WorkshopOS is designed for self-hosted deployment with PostgreSQL. You are responsible for infrastructure, TLS, backups, secrets, patching, and operational security.

## Is there license-key DRM?

**No technical DRM** in v1.0.0-rc1. Licensing is contractual. No phone-home activation, remote kill switch, or license telemetry is included.

## Is WorkshopOS open source?

**No.** Owner direction is a **proprietary commercial source license**, not MIT/Apache/GPL or similar open-source licensing for WorkshopOS itself.

## Is this FAQ legally binding?

**No.** It summarizes preferred commercial intent. Only your executed agreement and final license instrument are binding.
