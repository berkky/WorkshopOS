## Round II strategy shift

Round I optimized for premium SaaS morphology and produced five finalists (**Vernix, Apexon, Vantel, Nuvora, Velion**) that failed preliminary external commercial screening due to active technology/automotive/software collisions, occupied domains, and crowded `-ix`/`-on`/`-ora` patterns.

Round II optimizes in this order:

1. **Distinctiveness**
2. **Low obvious collision probability**
3. Premium feel
4. Memorability
5. Enterprise credibility
6. EN/TR pronunciation
7. Automotive-operations fit (abstract)

**Morphology discipline:** Fewer formulaic constructions built primarily from Apex-, Nova-/Nuv-, Vel-, Vant-, Vern-, Nex-, -ix, -ion, -on, -ora. Candidates relying mainly on those patterns received a distinctiveness penalty.
