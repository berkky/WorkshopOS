## Round III design philosophy

Round III optimizes **brand quality** over collision resistance alone. Round I and Round II morphology is explicitly retired.

### Quality north star

Names must feel credible beside disciplined premium technology companies (brand discipline reference set: Linear, Palantir, Anduril, Rivian, Celonis, Stripe, Verkada, Samsara, Lucid, Notion — **not naming sources**).

### What Round III rejects

- Nordic surnames and place-name cadence (`-lund`, `-sund`, `-mald`, `-mast`, `-vast`)
- Round II suffix bias (`-var`, `-ven`, `-vel`)
- Fantasy/RPG and gaming tone
- Random consonant piles
- Cheap startup morphology (`-ix`, `-ify`, `-ai`, `-io`, `-ora`, `-nova`, `-nex`, `-apex`, `-core`, `-flow`)
- Crypto, pharmaceutical, and generic agency tone
- Forced Q/X/Z decoration

### What Round III optimizes (weighted)

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|-------:|
| Distinctiveness | 20% |
| Premium feel | 18% |
| Memorability | 15% |
| Enterprise credibility | 12% |
| Collision-resistance heuristic | 12% |
| EN/TR pronunciation | 8% |
| Spelling clarity | 5% |
| Wordmark quality | 5% |
| Product-family extensibility | 3% |
| Automotive fit (abstract) | 2% |

**Red-flag override:** Obvious resemblance to major automotive OEMs, workshop software, ERP/CRM vendors, large SaaS companies, industrial tool manufacturers, major pharmaceuticals, or financial brands disqualifies a candidate regardless of aesthetic score.

## Internal candidate generation

**Internal pool size:** 132 fresh candidates (excluding all Round I, Round II, and comparison-control names).

**Generation constraints:** 5–8 letters (9 only if exceptional); 2–3 syllables; one word; no hyphens or numbers; EN/TR pronounceable; no Turkish special characters required.

**Filter pipeline:**

```
132 candidates
  → morphology penalty filter (Round I/II patterns, startup suffixes)
  → internal collision-heuristic filter (common-knowledge only)
  → EN/TR semantic screen (obvious negative/vulgar/comic associations)
  → wordmark + hero + product-family phrasing test
  → Top 30
  → premium-quality gate
  → Final 12 (no winner selected)
```

**Not exposed:** Full raw 132-candidate pool (per STEP 24.4 reporting rules).
