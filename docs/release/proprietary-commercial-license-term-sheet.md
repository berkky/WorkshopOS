## 3. Product

**WorkshopOS Commercial Source Edition** — proprietary automotive workshop management software, including delivered source code, documentation, and brand assets associated with the licensed release.

## 4. Ownership

WorkshopOS licensor retains ownership of WorkshopOS intellectual property, including base source code, architecture, original UI/UX, documentation, and proprietary modifications, unless explicitly assigned in writing.

Source delivery does **not** transfer copyright ownership.

## 5. License grant (preferred)

Non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as expressly approved), non-sublicensable (except as expressly approved), limited license to the **named legal entity** for use within the **licensed organizational and location scope**.

## 6. Permitted use (preferred)

- Install and run WorkshopOS within licensed scope
- Use for internal business operations of the licensed organization
- Access delivered source code for licensed internal purposes
- Create reasonable internal backups
- Modify source for licensed internal use and deploy modifications within licensed scope

## 7. Source-code access

Source may be delivered to the licensee for operational, maintenance, and internal development purposes subject to confidentiality and use restrictions.

## 8. Modification rights (preferred)

Licensee may modify WorkshopOS for **licensed internal use**. Modifications do not grant redistribution rights to the original WorkshopOS code.

**DERIVATIVE WORK / CUSTOMER MODIFICATION OWNERSHIP: LEGAL COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED**

Possible contractual models (neutral examples only):

- Licensor retains base IP; licensee owns bespoke customizations separately documented
- Licensee receives limited rights in customizations for internal use only
- Reusable product enhancements revert or remain with licensor unless otherwise agreed

## 9. Deployment / location scope (preferred)

License unit: **licensed legal entity + licensed workshop/location allowance**.

Not primarily per-user or per-technician seat licensing.

Additional locations require separate commercial entitlement.

## 10. Contractor access (preferred)

Employees and approved contractors/agencies may access source when reasonably required, provided confidentiality and license restrictions continue to apply through written obligations.

## 11. Restrictions (preferred)

Without separate written permission, licensee should **not**:

- Resell or redistribute WorkshopOS source code
- Sublicense WorkshopOS
- Publish proprietary source publicly
- Upload proprietary source to public repositories (GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket public repos, etc.)
- Provide source to unrelated third parties
- Offer WorkshopOS itself as a standalone competing product to unrelated customers
- Remove ownership/copyright notices
- Represent themselves as creator/owner of WorkshopOS
- Use WorkshopOS trademarks beyond agreed branding rights

Restrictions should not prevent legitimate internal customization within licensed scope.

## 12. Redistribution

No general redistribution rights. Internal deployment within licensed scope only.

## 13. Resale / white-label (preferred)

**Not included** in standard Commercial Source Edition.

White-label, reseller, partner, or OEM rights require a **separate written agreement** and separate commercial terms.

## 14. Customer data (preferred)

**Software IP** and **customer operational data** are separate.

Customer retains rights/ownership in its business data subject to contract and applicable law. WorkshopOS software ownership does not transfer ownership of customer data to the licensor.

No privacy-law compliance guarantee is implied by this term sheet.

## 15. Customer modifications

See Section 8. Final ownership, support impact, and reuse of improvements require legal review.

## 16. Third-party components

Third-party/open-source components remain governed by their own licenses. See [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](../../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) and bundled license files.

WorkshopOS proprietary terms do not override third-party license obligations.

**DM Sans** is self-hosted under **SIL OFL 1.1** — WorkshopOS does not claim exclusive ownership of DM Sans.

## 17. Brand / trademark (preferred)

License does not automatically transfer WorkshopOS trademark, logo, or brand ownership.

Licensee may display WorkshopOS branding as necessary to operate the product unless white-label rights are separately granted.

## 18. Confidentiality (preferred)

Delivered proprietary source is confidential commercial material. Access limited to personnel/contractors with reasonable need and appropriate confidentiality obligations.

This term sheet is not a full NDA.

## 19. Backup / disaster recovery (preferred)

Reasonable internal backups of licensed source and binaries permitted for business continuity, disaster recovery, and development operations.

## 20. Maintenance / updates (preferred)

**Perpetual use** applies to the **licensed version(s) delivered**.

Maintenance/update entitlement (if purchased) provides access to future product updates/upgrades during the maintenance term. Updates are **not** automatically perpetual.

## 21. Support (preferred)

Support is separate from the base software license and provided according to purchased support tier and agreement scope.

## 22. Custom development (preferred)

Customer-specific work is separate from the base license. Categories:

| Category | Typical treatment |
|----------|-------------------|
| A. Configuration / customization | Customer-scoped |
| B. Customer-specific extension | Contract-defined |
| C. Reusable platform enhancement | Normally remains WorkshopOS IP unless otherwise agreed in writing |
