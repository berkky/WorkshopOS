## Forbidden naming patterns

### Generic workshop terminology (primary brand stem)

Do not use as the brand word: Auto-, Garage-, Workshop-, Repair-, Service-, Torque-, Mechanic-, Shop-, Fleet- (as primary stem).

### Lazy startup morphology

Avoid as primary construction device: Nova-, Nuv-, Apex-, Nex-, and formulaic suffixes `-ify`, `-ai`, `-io`, `-verse`, crowded `-ix`, `-on`, `-ora`.

### Round I–III retired morphology

Avoid regenerating: `-var`, `-sund`, `-ven`, `-vel`, `-lund`, `-mast`, `-mald`, `-vast`, Nordic surname cadence, fantasy/RPG tone.

### Derivative recycling

Do not produce names mainly by changing one letter of a rejected finalist (e.g. Lumaro → Lumera; Cordel → Kordel). See [rejected-commercial-brand-register.md](rejected-commercial-brand-register.md).

### Forced decoration

No hyphens, numbers, or forced Q/X/Z decoration for distinctiveness.

## Domain policy

Domain is **secondary** to brand and legal quality.

| Priority | Pattern | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | `<brand>.com` | Preferred if commercially sensible after clearance |
| 2 | `<brand>.app` | Acceptable for product/SaaS positioning |
| 3 | `<brand>.io` | Acceptable for technology audience |
| 4 | `get<brand>.com` | Marketing fallback only |

- Do **not** create awkward spelling solely to obtain exact `.com`
- Do **not** purchase domains until brand cleared and owner authorizes
- Configure `security@<official-domain>` only after final domain selected

## Brand selection gate

A future brand must **not** be selected solely because:

- it sounds premium in isolation
- it scores highly in AI ranking
- a `.com` appears available in a quick check
- Google returns few results today

**Required before selection:**

1. Commercial collision screening (stages 2–5)
2. Official trademark database screening (stages 7–10)
3. Counsel confusing-similarity review (stage 12)
4. **Owner written approval**
5. Rebrand authorization per [commercial-rebrand-playbook.md](commercial-rebrand-playbook.md)
